# 💊 Treatments Tab - User Guide

## Overview

The **Evidence-Based Treatments** tab provides a comprehensive resource of proven mental health interventions for youth. This guide helps you navigate and understand the treatment options.

## Getting Started

1. Click the **"💊 Evidence-Based Treatments"** tab at the top of the dashboard
2. You'll see a treatment browser with filtering options and treatment cards
3. Click any treatment card to learn more details

## Filtering Treatments

### Filter Options

**1. Mental Health Condition**
- **All Treatments** (default)
- **Depression** - Treatments specifically for depressive disorders
- **Anxiety** - Treatments for anxiety disorders
- **Self-Harm** - Treatments targeting self-harm behaviors

**2. Treatment Category**
- Shows all available categories based on your condition selection
- Categories include: Psychotherapy, Medication, Family Therapy, Support, etc.

**3. Age Group**
- **All Age Groups** (default)
- **9-12 years** - Elementary/Middle school youth
- **13-17 years** - High school teens
- **18-25 years** - Young adults

### Example Filters

**To find treatments for teen depression:**
1. Condition: Depression
2. Age Group: 13-17
3. Result: See all proven depression treatments for teenagers

**To find family-involved treatments:**
1. Category: Family Therapy
2. Result: See treatments involving parents/guardians

## Understanding Treatment Cards

Each treatment card shows:

### Key Information
- **Treatment Name** - Title and category badge
- **Description** - Brief overview of what the treatment is
- **Effectiveness** - Percentage based on research (higher is better)
  - 80-90%: Highly effective
  - 70-79%: Effective
  - 60-69%: Moderately effective

### Details Shown
- **Conditions Treated** - Which mental health issues it helps
- **Age Groups** - Who it's designed for
- **Duration** - How long typical treatment lasts
- **Key Benefit** - The primary advantage of this treatment

### Color Coding

**Effectiveness Bar Colors:**
- 🟢 Green (80%+): Gold standard treatments
- 🟡 Orange (70-79%): Highly recommended
- 🔵 Blue (Below 70%): Supportive/preventive

## Treatment Categories Explained

### Psychotherapy (Talk Therapy)
- **CBT**: Changes thought patterns and behaviors
- **DBT**: Best for self-harm and emotional regulation
- **IPT**: Focuses on relationships and social support
- **ACT**: Teaches accepting difficult thoughts while pursuing values

**Typical Outcome**: 12-20 sessions over 3-6 months

### Medication (Primarily SSRIs)
- First-line antidepressants for youth
- FDA-approved for depression and anxiety
- **Always combine with therapy** for best results
- Takes 4-6 weeks to feel effects

### Family Therapy
- Involves parents/guardians in treatment
- Improves family communication
- Most effective for younger youth
- **Best for** ages 9-17

### Integrated Treatment (Gold Standard)
- **Combination of therapy + medication**
- Shows best outcomes in research
- Recommended for moderate to severe cases
- 85% effectiveness rate

### Support Services
- **Peer Support Groups**: Share experiences with others
- **Crisis Lines**: 24/7 immediate help (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
- **School-Based Programs**: Prevention and early intervention

### Lifestyle Interventions
- **Exercise**: 30+ min, 5x/week (as effective as medication for mild cases!)
- **Sleep**: Consistent schedule and sleep hygiene
- **Nutrition**: Healthy diet supporting brain health
- **Low/no cost** and **no side effects**

### Social Skills Training
- Learn communication and relationship skills
- Reduce social anxiety and isolation
- Often part of larger therapy package

### Crisis Intervention
- Emergency support during acute crises
- Prevents suicide and severe self-harm
- Available 24/7 via phone/text

## Common Questions

### "Which treatment should I choose?"

**Start here based on severity:**

**Mild symptoms:**
→ Lifestyle changes + CBT or family therapy + peer support

**Moderate symptoms:**
→ Add SSRIs to psychotherapy (combination approach)

**Severe symptoms:**
→ Combination therapy + medication, possibly hospitalization

**Self-harm behavior:**
→ DBT is specifically designed for this

### "Can I start with just lifestyle changes?"

**Yes, for mild-moderate symptoms:**
- Exercise is as effective as antidepressants for mild depression
- Combined with therapy, lifestyle changes enhance outcomes
- **But**: If symptoms don't improve in 2-3 weeks, add professional help

### "Do medications have side effects?"

**SSRIs (most common):**
- Generally well-tolerated in youth
- Common: nausea, sleep changes, appetite changes
- Serious side effects are rare
- Always supervised by psychiatrist

**Note**: Black box warning about suicidal thoughts early in treatment—requires monitoring

### "How long does treatment take?"

**Varies by treatment:**
- **Psychotherapy**: 12-20 sessions (3-6 months typical)
- **Medication**: Ongoing, at least 6-12 months after symptom resolution
- **Combination**: Usually shows improvement within 4-8 weeks
- **Lifestyle changes**: 6-8 weeks to see effects

### "What if my first treatment doesn't work?"

**This is normal:**
- 30-40% don't respond to first treatment tried
- Switch to different treatment type
- Increase dose or intensity
- Combine with other approaches
- Work closely with provider on adjustments

## Crisis Resources

**If in immediate crisis:**
- **Call/Text 988** - Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
- **Text HOME to 741741** - Crisis Text Line
- **Go to nearest Emergency Room** - For self-harm or suicidal thoughts
- **Call 911** - For medical emergencies
