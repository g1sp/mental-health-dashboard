## By Age Group

### 👧👦 AGES 9-12 (Elementary/Middle School)

**Best for this age:**
- Family-Based Treatment (parents involved)
- CBT (with parental involvement)
- Exercise & Physical Activity
- Sleep Hygiene
- School-Based Programs
- Social Skills Training

**Why different?** Need parental involvement; developing cognitive skills

### 👨🎓👩🎓 AGES 13-17 (High School)

**Best for this age:**
- ANY of the treatments!
- Start with: CBT or Family Therapy
- If more severe: Add SSRIs
- If self-harm: DBT
- Crisis support: 988 (call/text)

**Why this age?** Peak onset of many mental health conditions

### 👨👩 AGES 18-25 (Young Adults)

**Best for this age:**
- Individual psychotherapies
- All medications
- Peer support groups
- MBCT (Mindfulness)
- Combination treatment
- Lifestyle interventions

**Why this age?** More autonomous decision-making; can engage in complex therapies

## By Treatment Type

### 💬 TALK THERAPY (Psychotherapy)
**How:** Work with a therapist 1-on-1
**Duration:** 12-20 sessions over 3-6 months
**Cost:** Varies ($, $$)
**Best For:** Most conditions
**Options:**
- CBT: Changing thoughts & behaviors (80% effective)
- DBT: Emotional regulation (85% for self-harm)
- IPT: Relationships & social (75%)
- ACT: Acceptance & values (72%)

### 💊 MEDICATION
**How:** Antidepressants (SSRIs)
**Duration:** 4-6 weeks to feel effects; months/years to maintain
**Cost:** Usually $; generic available
**Best For:** Moderate-severe depression/anxiety
**Note:** ALWAYS combine with therapy for best results

### 👨‍👩‍👧 FAMILY THERAPY
**How:** Parents/guardians involved in treatment
**Duration:** 12-16 sessions
**Cost:** $$
**Best For:** Ages 9-17; family stressors
**Helps:** Communication, support, understanding

### 🏥 INTEGRATED TREATMENT
**How:** Therapy + Medication together (Gold Standard)
**Duration:** Ongoing (months to years)
**Cost:** $$ (therapy + meds)
**Best For:** Moderate-severe conditions
**Why Best:** 85% effectiveness rate

### 💪 LIFESTYLE
**How:** Self-directed changes
**Options:**
- Exercise: 30+ min, 5x/week (Free)
- Sleep: Consistent schedule (Free)
- Nutrition: Healthy eating (Free-$)
**Effectiveness:** 50-68%
**As effective as:** Medication for mild depression!

### 👥 GROUP/SUPPORT
**How:** Connect with others
**Options:**
- Peer support groups
- School programs
- Crisis lines: 988
**Cost:** Free-low cost
**Best For:** Support, reducing isolation, hope

### 🚨 CRISIS
**How:** 24/7 emergency support
**Methods:** Call 988, Text HOME→741741, ER
**Cost:** Free
**For:** Immediate help with suicidal thoughts, acute crisis

## Finding The Right Treatment

### 🚦 QUICK DECISION TREE

**Do you have thoughts of suicide or self-harm?**
→ YES: Call 988 NOW (Crisis Line)
→ NO: Continue below

**How severe are symptoms?**

**MILD** (Occasional sadness/worry, manageable)
- Try: Exercise + Sleep + Peer Support
- Then: Add CBT if no improvement in 2-3 weeks

**MODERATE** (Regular symptoms, interfering with school/life)
- Start: CBT or Family Therapy
- Add if needed: SSRI medication
- Monitor: Every 2-4 weeks with provider

**SEVERE** (Daily symptoms, can't function, self-harm/suicidal thoughts)
- Get: Professional help ASAP
- Combine: Therapy + Medication
- Consider: DBT (for self-harm)
- If crisis: Go to ER or call 988

## Timeline: What to Expect

### Week 1: Starting Treatment
- Intake appointment
- Assessment of symptoms
- Treatment plan discussion
- Start therapy and/or medication

### Weeks 2-4: Early Phase
- Regular therapy appointments
- If medication: May feel slight effects (4-6 weeks typical)
- Coping strategies introduction
- Symptom tracking

### Weeks 5-8: Adjustment Phase
- Medication: Full effects usually felt
- Therapy: Working on core issues
- Expect: Good days and still-hard days
- **Important**: Don't give up if not instant improvement!

### Months 2-3: Improvement Phase
- Noticeable improvements in mood/anxiety
- Learning to apply coping skills
- Increasing social engagement
- Better sleep and energy

### Months 3-6: Consolidation Phase
- Continued improvement
- Building resilience
- Preparing for independence
- Discussing maintenance plan

### 6+ Months: Maintenance
- Reduced session frequency
- Continued medication if helpful
- Independent coping skills
- Relapse prevention planning

## Red Flags: When to Get Help NOW

🚨 **Call 988 (Crisis Line) or Go to ER if:**
- Suicidal thoughts
- Active self-harm
- Unable to function (can't eat, sleep, go to school)
- Severe anxiety/panic
- Hearing voices or hallucinating
- Substance abuse emerging

⚠️ **Call Provider Within 24 Hours if:**
- Sudden worsening of symptoms
- New self-harm urges
- Medication side effects are bad
- Thoughts of harming others
- Major life event or loss

📞 **Schedule Appointment This Week if:**
- Symptoms lasting 2+ weeks
- Interfering with school/work
- Not feeling better with current treatment
- Want to discuss treatment options

## Success Tips

✅ **Give treatment time** (4-8 weeks minimum)
✅ **Attend all appointments** (consistency matters)
✅ **Try assigned homework** (skills need practice)
✅ **Track symptoms** (helps see progress)
✅ **Be honest** with your provider
✅ **Tell provider about side effects** (often fixable)
✅ **Ask questions** (you're the expert on your experience)
✅ **Involve trusted adults** (parents, school counselor)
✅ **Practice coping skills** between sessions
✅ **Be patient with yourself** (healing takes time)
